There has been much recent work (CuLifter, NVLift) in developing reverse-engineering tooling to lift NVIDIA SASS code to typed LLVM IR. AMD 
binaries lack this tooling despite utilizing LLVM-IR as a forward compilation intermediate step. This project aims to address this divide by 
developing a pipeline to lift AMD binaries to LLVM IR as a typed, human-readable intermediate layer. Our pipeline operates on disassembled AMD RDNA4 
binaries (generated by llvm-objdump) by performing semantic and heuristic analysis for control flow reconstruction by generating a CFG and 
lifting it to LLVM IR.  We then compile the LLVM IR back to AMD v-code and fuzz both this binary and the original to compare the accuracy of
the reconstructed control flow. We are specifically using fuzzing not only to verify semantic correctness, but also to verify that our methodology 
of wavefront-divergence lifting is sound and tractable.
